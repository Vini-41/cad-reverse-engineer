# SKY-001 (Skyfall AI)

Researched 2026-10-26.

**Bottom line.** SKY-001 exists. It is a model from [Skyfall AI](https://skyfall.ai/), a startup founded by the Maluuba co-founders, and it was announced on 2026-10-19 as a "research preview". The only way to get it is a waitlist. It is not an LLM. Skyfall calls it a "spatial world model": diffusion models that take a product image and generate a part tree, then predict a sequence of *visual* 3D states and 2D sketch canvases for building each part. The company and its people are real, with traceable prior papers and code. The model, though, cannot yet be checked by anyone outside Skyfall: there are no weights, API, license, pricing, technical report or released benchmark. Every number about it is self-reported, and the maker's own pages disagree with each other in places. It is not relevant to cadrev now. For use (a) it is no candidate at all: it is not a language model, has no tool use and cannot be accessed. For use (b) it is at most a future comparison point. It works from images, scores itself by IoU against a reference shape, and says editable feature histories are still to come. So no dimension it produces traces to a source, which is the bar the [LLM-CAD section](cadrev-adjacent-tools.md#4-prior-art-cad-from-papers-drawings-and-images-llm-cad-and-its-verification) of the adjacent-tools note sets.

A search for "Sky-001", "Sky 001", "SKY-001" and "Sky001" turned up nothing else that shares the name: no other model, paper or arXiv entry ([arXiv API query](https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?search_query=all:%22SKY-001%22)). ## 2. What it is, by the maker's account

- **Not an LLM.** From the FAQ: "SKY-001 is World Model Based and doesn't do next-token prediction like LLMs. Given a prompt or 2D image, it uses a hierarchical composer which decides what the design is made of" ([skyfall.ai/sky-001](https://skyfall.ai/sky-001)).
- **Two stages** ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §2–4):
  - A **Hierarchical Composer**, built on DiT diffusion. It "first generates a rough 3D shape of the whole object", splits it into assemblies and then parts, and a refiner fills in each leaf's hidden geometry.
  - A **Construction World Model**. For CAD it "plans the intermediate 3D states of a part, and between each pair of consecutive states it draws the 2D sketch that turns one into the next". The 2D canvases are rolled out in a Matryoshka-style latent space and then refined.
- **Its output is visual.** The pipeline's output is a "visual design plan" of "ordered visual states" ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §2). The post itself warns: "Transitions are illustrative, and the saved sketch and 3D geometry can disagree" (§4).
- **Editable CAD is future work.** "In CAD, sketch rollouts would become executable operations with an editable feature history" ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §7, "What Comes Next"). The press release says it "plans complete, executable Computer-Aided Design (CAD) trajectories" and names Autodesk Fusion as an example environment ([WebWire](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=363053)). That claim goes further than the technical post, which never shows a Fusion file or any other CAD file.
- **Training data.** The training data, by the maker's account ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §5):
  - It comes from "publicly available online datasets of real product assemblies", cleaned up by Skyfall. The datasets are not named, apart from [PartVerse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dscdyc/partverse), which is used only as a point of comparison.
  - Where construction histories were missing, "engineering tools and LLM-based workflows reverse-engineer construction trajectories".
  - The composer learns from more than 200,000 parts, and the construction model from over 600,000 construction examples.
- **Broader claims.** The site also pitches SKY-001 for "PCB design, aerospace, energy, protein folding, molecules, computer use agents and enterprise software", and says it designs objects "with hundreds of thousands of parts" ([skyfall.ai/sky-001](https://skyfall.ai/sky-001)). The launch post shows CAD and BIM only.

## 4. Benchmarks, as the maker reports them

All the benchmarks below are Skyfall's own and have not been released. I found no independent evaluation.

- **CAD construction.** The benchmark is "1,000 components sampled from complex objects such as a Jaguar E-Type Roadster, a FANUC ArcMate 100iB robotic arm…", built in "about 100 drawing steps", with "the final 3D object … scored by IoU against the reference" ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §6). Results: SKY-001 79.7% IoU, 3.9 s and $0.005 per case; GPT-6 Astra 79.1%, 199 s and $0.75. The post itself calls this "matching its performance".
- **BIM construction.** 1,000 floor plans. SKY-001 43.6% IoU, 1.22 s; GPT-6 Astra 40.6%, 144.6 s ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §6).
- **Composer.** The baselines were frontier LLMs "each writing a Blender script for the whole assembly in one request", and two LLM judges scored renders from 1 to 10 ([launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001), §6).
- **What the post leaves out.** It gives no baseline prompts or harness for the construction benchmarks, no seeds or variance, no hardware, and no basis for the cost figures.
- **The maker's pages disagree:**
  - The CAD table on [skyfall.ai/sky-001](https://skyfall.ai/sky-001) lists Opus 5.5 at 69.4% IoU and Gemini 3.8 Flash at 54.7%. The [launch post](https://skyfall.ai/blog/introducing-sky-001)'s CAD chart lists "Gemini 4" at 76.9% and has no Opus row. Small BIM figures also differ, for example 144.4 s against 144.6 s for GPT-6 Astra.
  - The [press release](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=363053) compares against "GPT-4, Gemini, Claude Opus, Grok", which are different models from those on the site.
  - Grok 4.7 is shown with a time and a cost although it "completed none" of the CAD cases.

## 5. Legitimacy

- **For the company:** an identifiable company, founders verified through Microsoft's own post, a dated release issued by the maker, a prior arXiv and GitHub record, and a launch post that describes its method in some detail.
- **Against the model, as of today:**
  - It cannot be reproduced: no weights, API, report or benchmark.
  - Every number is self-reported, and the maker's pages contradict each other.
  - The site looks unfinished. Each founder's role reads `Co-founder · [Title]`, the Technical report link is `#`, and the Blog nav link points at a temporary `trycloudflare.com` tunnel ([skyfall.ai](https://skyfall.ai/) bundle).
  - The marketing claims run far beyond what the post shows: "hundreds of thousands of parts", proteins, and "AI that can 'dream'".
- **No scam signs found:** no crypto or token tie-in, no paid signup.

Verdict: a real company's research preview with an unverified marketing layer, not a scam. Treat its performance claims as unverified until a report or access exists.

## 6. Relevance to cadrev

- **(a) As the agent that drives the skill: no.** The skill needs a model that reads sources, quotes verbatim, writes build123d and runs `cadrev`. SKY-001 is not a language model, has no tool use and cannot be accessed.
- **(b) As a geometry generator to compare against or feed: not now, and weak in principle.**
  - Its input is an image or a brief. cadrev's evidence is a hashed source whose dimensions are quoted.
  - Its output is visual states, and the maker warns that sketch and geometry "can disagree". cadrev needs a parametric solid whose params trace to the ledger.
  - It is scored by IoU against a reference. As the [adjacent-tools judgement](cadrev-adjacent-tools.md#4-prior-art-cad-from-papers-drawings-and-images-llm-cad-and-its-verification) notes, that is similarity to a ground truth cadrev does not have.
  - A dimension SKY-001 inferred from a picture would be an untraced number, exactly what the ledger exists to prevent. At best it could *propose* a part tree that a human or the agent then grounds in quotes, much as figure readings await confirmation.
- **Trigger to revisit:** Skyfall ships editable feature-history output (STEP or a parametric history) with API or weights access, and a technical report with a public benchmark. Even then the fit would sit in the place [Recommendation 4](cadrev-adjacent-tools.md#recommendations-ranked-by-value) gives mesh-distance checks: useful only where a source ships geometry.
