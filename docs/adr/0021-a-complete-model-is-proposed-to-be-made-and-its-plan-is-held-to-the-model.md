# A complete model is proposed to be made, and its plan is held to the model

A reconstruction is meant to be made, not only modified and simulated: ADR-0007 first ranked fabrication last and kept it out. A complete model settles three foundational questions before it proposes anything: metric or US, the material (what the made parts are made of and how, or none yet), and design or function. project.yaml records them as `units.length`, `material` and `follows`, and the report prints them. Design builds the object as its sources show it. Function replaces a feature that is hard to make in the material with one that does the same job, the replacement's rationale naming what it replaces, and a stated value left unbuilt set aside as `not-modeled` with that reason. Whatever is proposed is chosen to be made: a standard size, a bought part before a made one, a fit as loose as its function allows.

Once a design is accepted, its plan says how each part is made, what is bought, and the steps that assemble the instances, in `plan.yaml`. cadrev holds the plan to the built model: every part an instance places is made or bought, every instance is placed by exactly one step, each thing a step drives is a thread param's, and where holes threaded for a screw outnumber the screws driven a warning says so. The report prints the plan with each quantity counted from the model. `tolerance` stays the model's, how closely it realises a value; what a shop works to is the plan's to state.

This came from a mechanical engineer's review of a run on a patent: nothing asked the units, the material or whether to follow the design; the model invented sizes no catalogue holds; and a reconstruction is usually wanted as a product that can be made, so it should end in a fabrication and assembly sequence.

We rejected holding design rules in cadrev: a wall's least thickness, a fit's clearance and what a process can form depend on the material, the machine and the part's job, and a table of them would be wrong as often as right. They are judgement, which the skills ask of the model and the report shows as proposed values with their rationale. Threads and screw lengths are held (ADR-0018) because their tables are exact and every assembly has them. We rejected the plan as prose beside the project: nothing would hold it to the model, and it would name parts and count screws the model no longer has. We rejected checking that a step can be done, by sweeping each instance into place: a screw in its tapped hole, a press fit and a snap fit all overlap on the way in by design, so the check would fail the joints that work. We rejected tolerance analysis, and tolerances on the drawings (ADR-0019): the plan states what each part is made to, in words.
