# A lead screw is a gear across joint types, not a screw joint

A lead screw couples a revolute joint and a slide: a gear stated by `lead` in place of `ratio`, a length param giving how far the slide moves per full turn of the revolute joint, negative where the slide moves toward its axis's first datum as the revolute joint turns positive. "8 mm per revolution" is ledgered as an ordinary 8 mm, so no new count unit is needed. It exports as the same MJCF joint equality as a ratio gear, at lead ÷ 2π in the length unit per radian, so MuJoCo poses what cadrev poses (ADR-0008). A posed `distance` check with `from` compares the carriage's travel between two poses with a stated value, so a wrong sign, a unit slip or a slide axis that is not the screw's fails it. A posed check reads the ratio or lead of each gear its pose moves a joint through, along any chain, as it reads a cable's turn and closed values (ADR-0009): the param joins its Reads and becomes checked. A rack and pinion is the same gear, its lead the pinion's pitch circumference.

We rejected a helical `screw` joint. The cell stretcher's screw turns in bearings while a separate carriage slides on rails (Kah et al. 2021), so these are two instances and two joints, and MJCF has no helical joint to export one to. We rejected a lead as a ratio in mm per degree, which hides the quantity a source prints and lets a ratio pass as a length; and `ratio` across joint types, which would make a ratio's unit depend on its joints.
