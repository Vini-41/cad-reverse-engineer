# A released body's datum may be derived from its triangle corners

An STL holds only flat triangles, so a released STL gives no cylindrical face to read a diameter from and no axis to put a datum on. COMPAct's 13-sided sun bore has no two corners opposite each other, so its longest chord reads 4.9635 for 5 (#136); a hole's centre lies off the solid; a fillet's radius cannot be probed (#143). So a datum on a released body may be derived in evidence.yaml from `[x, y, z]` datums declared on the same body: `{circle: [a, b, c]}`, the circle through three, and `{mid: [a, b]}`, the midpoint of two. A `diameter` probe at a circle datum reads the circle's diameter; a `distance` or `angle` probe, or a shape check's pair, reads it at its centre. Three corners of the sun's rim give 5 to the file's float precision, whichever three are taken.

Each datum it names is confirmed on the solid as ADR-0006 confirms any datum, and the derived point is not: a centre lies in air by design. On an STL each one must also lie within `tolerance.length` of a triangle corner, since a corner is the only point a mesh stores on the true surface: a point mid-facet lies inside the circle by the facet's sag, and a point slid along a facet records wherever it was put. A STEP body's surface is exact, so its datums need only lie on it. Derived datums are declared on released bodies only, never in model.py, since a built part keeps real cylinders and axes. A `distance` or `angle` check reads the built model's datums, so a derived datum reaches a check only as a shape check's pair.

## Considered options

- **Finding features on the mesh** (fitting a bore to its facets, detecting holes). Rejected again, as in ADR-0006: it needs a classifier of facets into features with tolerances of its own, and a fit that cannot say which facets it took. Three named corners are reviewable and evaluate the same way every run. ADR-0006's gap stays open on a STEP body, where a datum slid along a face still passes; on an STL the corner rule closes it to a choice among stored corners.
- **`through: [a, b, c]` on a `diameter` probe** (#136). Rejected: a circle datum declared once serves the diameter and the centre that distances and angles need.
- **A circle fit through more than three points.** Rejected: three corners already recover the circle to float32 precision, and a fit adds a residual to judge.
- **A `distance` between opposite corners**, the skill's route for an STL bore. Rejected: an odd-sided rim has none.
