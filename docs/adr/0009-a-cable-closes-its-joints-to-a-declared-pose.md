# A cable closes its joints to a declared pose

A cable wound on a wheel joint moves each joint it closes in proportion to the wheel's turn, from the assembly as built to a declared closed pose, and the MJCF exports that as joint equalities, so MuJoCo poses exactly what cadrev poses (ADR-0008). Where no source states the closed pose, each joint's closed value is proposed with a gap, and a stated order of closing is not represented: a finger whose joints bend proximal first through spring preload is flattened by the linear model, and the closed values' interpretation says so. A stroke check reads the cable's path through datums on the posed model, so it tests the geometry rather than a winding radius computed from the stroke itself.

We rejected letting MuJoCo find where the joints come to rest, a spatial tendon wound on the wheel pulling against the joints' springs. That is behavioural simulation, which ADR-0007 keeps out: a failed check could not say whether the reconstruction or the solver's springs and contacts were wrong, and the posed model cadrev checks would no longer be the one MuJoCo runs. Per-joint ratios with no closed pose were rejected too: a closed angle is the question a source could answer, a bare ratio is not.

We removed the elastic tendon, a cord with a stiffness and a damping that the MJCF exported as a sprung spatial tendon. How far it stretches is behavioural simulation too, no check read it, and no project has declared one since its cords became cables. An elastic cord is a cable where its path is what a source states, and its stated stiffness is a statement the model leaves unbuilt.
