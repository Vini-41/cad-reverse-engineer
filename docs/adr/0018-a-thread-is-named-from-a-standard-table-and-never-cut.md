# A thread is named from a standard table and never cut

A screw thread enters a project as a thread param: a designation the ISO metric table (ISO 261's first- and second-choice diameters from M1 to M39, coarse and fine pitches) or the Unified one (ASME B1.1's UNC and UNF from #0 to 1 in) holds, in place of a value. A source's thread is the designation its quote or reading prints; a proposed one comes from the project's table, `threads: metric | unified` in project.yaml, which with the length unit is the user's answer to metric or US. The param reads as its major diameter and carries what its table gives: its pitch, its tap drill (major less pitch) and its clearance hole (ISO 273 medium, ASME B18.2.8 normal, each as its standard tabulates the hole; a size they list none for has none). model.py builds each hole and shank as a plain cylinder from those sizes and declares the thread at a datum on it; cadrev holds the cylinder to the thread, from its minor diameter to its major or at its clearance hole, and the report and the part's drawing carry the designation. A hole or shank is declared once, and a `count` check counts those a thread is declared on, so a stated number of screws is checked without a screw being a part.

This came from a mechanical engineer's review of v0.1.0: screws and threads should not be recreated, their sizes should not be made up when a US and a metric table exist, and one of the two should be chosen. It amends ADR-0007, which left stock parts and their standards out with a deferred `standard` basis. Threads are the one standard taken in; manufacturing tolerances stay out, and `tolerance` is still a modelling tolerance.

We rejected the `standard` basis. Which thread a hole takes is still stated or proposed, and the report must say which; only the sizes that follow from the designation are the standard's, and they need no basis of their own. We rejected cutting helical threads, which bloats the STEP, slows every boolean and shape check, and tells a machinist nothing a designation does not; and a diameter and a pitch as two params, which lets a pair no tap exists for pass as M3. We rejected holding only threaded holes: the clearance hole is where a made-up 3.2 or 3.5 used to be proposed with a gap no author would answer.
