# Number words are numbers

A ledger value matches a number word in its verbatim quote as the number the word names: `value: 8` matches "Eight tension springs". The words are the cardinals zero to ninety-nine, hyphenated compounds included; ordinals and words such as "pair" or "dozen" are not numbers. A number word followed by a unit ("six turns") is a stated quantity the coverage sweep lists, so the sweep and the matcher stay in step (ADR-0004). Until #27, words were not numbers, so a paper that states its counts in words left them proposed, and that alone kept the project from Exact although the source states every count. We chose this over a reading for a human to confirm, as figure readings have, because a number word is read the same way every time: the quote is verbatim and the word has one value, so the matcher can check it and a reviewer's confirmation would add nothing (ADR-0002 reserves PENDING REVIEW for transcriptions only a person can check).
